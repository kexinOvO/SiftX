Third-party source materials / 第三方源码材料

SiftX runs FFmpeg, yt-dlp and QuickJS-NG as separate local programs.
Their original licenses, notices and source archives remain under their
respective upstream licenses. Licensing of SiftX's own source is a separate decision.

The distribution ZIP contains SiftX-ThirdParty-Sources.zip beside SiftX.app.
It includes pinned source archives, checksums, upstream build scripts,
dependency locks and SiftX's integration/signing scripts. Extract its
INDEX.md, ThirdParty/README.md and ThirdParty/BUILDING.md for details.

If an application-only download is offered, the publisher must also offer
the same source package beside that version's application download, at no
additional charge. This notice does not substitute for actually supplying
the matching source package.

The application bundle includes ThirdParty-LICENSES.txt and the original
FFmpeg-LICENSE.txt, yt-dlp-LICENSE.txt, YouTube-THIRD-PARTY-LICENSES.txt and
QuickJS-LICENSE.txt. In Finder, choose Show Package Contents, then open
Contents/Resources to read them.

发布压缩包内，SiftX-ThirdParty-Sources.zip 与 SiftX.app 位于同一级目录。
源码包包含原始源码、许可证、版本与校验值、上游构建文件和集成脚本。
若提供单独的应用下载，请在同版本下载位置同时免费提供这份源码包。
许可证也可在应用包 Contents/Resources 中查看。
